A little lesson, played together

Each adventure follows a predictable routine: review sounds, hear a word and build it, change one sound, then read a tiny story. Open the grown-up prompt below each activity for exactly what to say.

Let your child try first. Give help when needed. Completion records activities, not assessed reading mastery. There is no speech recording or automatic reading assessment.

Phonics uses bundled sound clips. Whole-word and instruction audio uses the device’s English voice. If it cannot play, read the prompt aloud. Model story helper a as “uh”; stop sounds stay short, without an added “uh.” Pronunciations vary with accent.
