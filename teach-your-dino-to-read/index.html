Read together, one word at a time

Each story has four short pages, with two or three words on each page. Ask your child to try saying the words, then slowly drag the yellow marker from left to right to hear them.

The marker reads whole words, not individual letter sounds. Moving quickly queues the words in order. Move backward or tap a word to hear it again.

The story helper a is spoken as “uh.” Stories include simple words and some endings such as “naps” and “fits.” Choose a book with patterns your child knows; this is reading practice, not a complete phonics curriculum.

Audio uses the device’s English voice. A voice download or internet connection may be needed on some devices. If it cannot play, read the highlighted words together. The app does not listen to or assess your child. Bookmarks and stories finished are saved in this browser when storage is available.
